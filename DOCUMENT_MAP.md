## Role Corpus Boundary

- [Role Corpus Architecture](https://github.com/RusDavies/docs-management-practices/blob/master/ROLE_CORPUS_ARCHITECTURE.md) — corpus-level split between management/leadership roles and practitioner/operator roles, including terminology, authority boundaries, downstream agent-specialist scope rules, and the recommended separate `docs-practitioner-practices` corpus.
- [Role Scope Split Examples](https://github.com/RusDavies/docs-management-practices/blob/master/ROLE_SCOPE_SPLIT_EXAMPLES.md) — initial management-vs-practitioner/operator boundary examples for software engineering, quality, and HR.
- [docs-practitioner-practices](https://github.com/RusDavies/docs-practitioner-practices) — companion corpus for practitioner/operator role guidance, including software engineering, QA/test, HR operations, research, funding, learning/teaching, security vulnerability remediation, and legal practitioner examples.
- [docs-governance-practices](https://github.com/RusDavies/docs-governance-practices) — companion corpus for governance/advisory role guidance, including board members, advisors, committee/council members, sponsor/stewards, and governance templates.

### Role Coverage Summary

Current management-to-practitioner/operator coverage:

- 34 discipline files reviewed, including `DELIVERY_METHODOLOGIES.md` as a non-management coverage case.
- 22 direct practitioner/operator counterparts exist: AI landscape, architecture, compliance/regulatory, customer, data/analytics, funding, learning/teaching, legal, marketing/GTM, operations, portfolio, procurement, product, program, project, quality, research, resource/financial, sales, software engineering, support/service, and vendor/partner.
- 1 broader counterpart coverage case exists: security management maps to security operations and security vulnerability remediation practitioner roles.
- 3 adjacent-role handoff cases exist: delivery methodologies, people management, and technical debt management.
- 3 practitioner/operator counterparts remain missing in the companion corpus: knowledge operations, change enablement, and incident response.
- 5 management-only disciplines intentionally have no same-domain practitioner/operator counterpart: culture, risk, stakeholder, succession, and team management.

## Originality / Plagiarism Review

Before public release:

- [ ] Check that copied or closely paraphrased third-party text is absent or properly quoted/attributed.
- [ ] Check that named frameworks are discussed at a high level unless permission/quotation is tracked.
- [ ] Check that no diagrams/tables from third parties are copied without permission.
- [ ] Check that AI-assisted text has not introduced unattributed close paraphrase.
- [ ] Check that sensitive factual claims are sourced or removed.

## Attribution Notes

If publishing material that substantially references Lencioni-inspired ideas, consider a note such as:

> This guidance is informed in part by common team-health concepts popularized by Patrick Lencioni, including trust, productive conflict, commitment, accountability, and shared results. The guidance, examples, structure, and wording here are original.

Use only if it improves transparency and does not make the work look derivative.

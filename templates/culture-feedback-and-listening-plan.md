## Questions to Explore

- What behaviours are rewarded here?
- What behaviours are tolerated even though they cause harm?
- What are people afraid to say?
- Where do stated values and lived experience differ?
- Where are standards unclear or inconsistently enforced?
- Where are people excluded from information, opportunity, or credit?
- What should leaders stop, start, or continue?

## Safeguards

- Do not promise anonymity if it cannot be guaranteed.
- Do not use feedback tools for retaliation, surveillance, or individual labelling.
- Protect sensitive reports and route employee-relations, legal, compliance, safety, or harassment concerns appropriately.
- Explain limits clearly before collecting feedback.

## Usage Notes

- Listening without action damages trust faster than not asking at all. Efficient, in the worst possible way.
- Use AI only for aggregation or drafting summaries where privacy, consent, access controls, and human review are appropriate.
- Close the loop even when the answer is “not now” or “we chose a different tradeoff.”

## Example

After a reorganization, run listening sessions by function, synthesize themes about unclear decision rights and role anxiety, publish the three actions leaders will take, and schedule a follow-up review in 45 days.

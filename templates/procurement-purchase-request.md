## Usage Notes

- Use this before a purchase is treated as inevitable because someone loved a demo.
- Scale the evidence to the risk and cost. A trivial purchase should not need a novel; a critical supplier should not be approved from vibes and a quote.
- Pair with the approval threshold matrix, supplier evaluation scorecard, supplier risk questionnaire, and renewal calendar.

## Example

A department requesting a new analytics platform would record the business need, users, expected cost, data exposure, integration needs, security/privacy reviews, replacement plan for old tooling, and the renewal notice date before approval.

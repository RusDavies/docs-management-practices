## Usage Notes

- Name the model before the debate. Many team arguments are really arguments about the rules of the argument.
- Consensus is useful for some decisions and terrible for others. Do not make every choice hostage to universal emotional alignment.
- Record decisions that affect priorities, responsibilities, risk, customers, standards, or future disagreement.

## Example

A platform team may delegate implementation decisions to service owners, use advice process for architecture tradeoffs, require product input for customer commitments, and escalate risk acceptance above defined thresholds.

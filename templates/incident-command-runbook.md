# Incident Command Runbook

## Purpose

Give the incident commander a practical operating sequence for declaration, coordination, containment, communication, recovery, closure, and handoff.

## Key Command Questions

- What is the current impact?
- Is the impact growing, shrinking, or unknown?
- What is the safest containment action?
- What evidence must be preserved before recovery changes?
- What decision is blocked, and who can unblock it?
- Who needs an update before the next response interval?
- What assumptions are we making?
- What would cause a severity change?

## Usage Notes

- The commander's job is not to solve every technical problem. It is to keep decisions, owners, timing, and communications coherent.
- Keep updates factual. Optimism is not a mitigation.
- Pair with the role roster, escalation matrix, timeline/decision log, and corrective-action tracker.

## Example

For a production outage, the commander may set a 15-minute update cadence, assign recovery to the technical lead, route customer updates through support, preserve deployment evidence, and record rollback decisions before closure.

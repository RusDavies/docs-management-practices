How we surface disagreement before decisions and commit after decisions.

## Usage Notes

- Use when forming a team, repairing team clarity, or changing operating norms.
- Keep it short enough to be remembered.
- Review after major team, mission, leadership, or workload changes.
- Use it as a reference for decision rights, escalation, and disagreement norms.

## Example

A platform team charter might define its purpose, outcomes owned, service boundaries, decision rights, support model, communication cadence, escalation path, and disagree-and-commit norms.

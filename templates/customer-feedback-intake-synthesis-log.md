# Customer Feedback Intake and Synthesis Log

## Purpose

Capture customer feedback in a way that separates the customer problem from the requested solution, preserves context, aggregates patterns, and feeds product, support, operations, and strategy decisions.

## Usage Notes

- Do not let one loud customer become “the market” without evidence. Loud is a signal; it is not a segmentation model.
- Preserve the customer’s problem even when the requested solution is not right. Product learning usually lives in the problem, not the customer’s proposed implementation.
- Use this with product management, support, customer success, account management, and operations reviews.

## Example

Three customers request different reporting exports. The synthesis log may reveal the underlying problem is audit evidence preparation, leading to a reusable product capability rather than three bespoke export jobs and a future maintenance swamp.

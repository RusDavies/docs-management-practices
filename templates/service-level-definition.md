## Usage Notes

- Do not promise service levels the organization refuses to fund. That is not ambition; it is calendar-shaped fiction.
- Define both the target and the measurement source, or arguments will migrate to the dashboard.
- Review after sustained breaches, demand shifts, incidents, customer commitments, or capacity changes.

## Example

A support escalation service might define critical response within one hour during coverage, daily updates for high-priority open cases, escalation after two missed updates, and explicit limits when customer-provided logs are missing.

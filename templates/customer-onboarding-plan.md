## Usage Notes

- Onboarding success should mean customer value is beginning, not merely that access was enabled and everyone wandered away.
- Use this for new customers, major expansions, migrations, or re-onboarding after serious recovery work.
- Review sales promises early; surprises discovered after kickoff are just small disasters with calendar invites.

## Example

For an analytics product, onboarding may include data-source access, security approval, dashboard configuration, user training, first executive report, support context, adoption metrics, and a 30-day value review.

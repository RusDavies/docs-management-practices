## Usage Notes

- Culture change needs behaviour, reinforcement, and consequences. Communication alone is just weather.
- Do not attempt to change everything at once. Pick the few patterns that most damage trust, delivery, safety, or customer outcomes.
- Re-check lived experience, not only leader confidence.

## Example

If the organization wants to move from heroic firefighting to reliable execution, change recognition, planning, incident reviews, staffing, and executive questions. Otherwise the old culture will eat the new slogan and ask for dessert.

# Change Go / No-Go Checklist

## Purpose

Support a deliberate rollout decision by checking readiness, risks, decision ownership, evidence, and conditions before a change proceeds.

## Usage Notes

- Do not let “green enough” become a ritual phrase for ignoring red evidence.
- Record who accepted conditions and what evidence will prove they were closed.
- Use this with the readiness assessment; the checklist is the decision moment, not a substitute for investigation.

## Example

A go-live may proceed with conditions if training for one low-impact group lands next week, but should delay if support coverage, customer notices, or critical data migration checks are not ready.

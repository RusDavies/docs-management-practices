# Knowledge Domain Map

## Purpose

Map the important knowledge domains in a team, function, product, service, or organization. Use this to clarify ownership, sources of truth, review expectations, access boundaries, and continuity risks.

## Usage Notes

- Start with domains that affect operations, support, onboarding, customer trust, risk, compliance, or continuity.
- Name real owners. “The team” is not an owner; it is a fog machine.
- Use this map to prioritize source-of-truth cleanup, lifecycle policies, onboarding packs, and AI retrieval eligibility.

## Example

A product team might map domains for release process, pricing exceptions, customer commitments, operational runbooks, incident response, vendor dependencies, and product architecture. The release process may be critical, high volatility, and owned by product operations with engineering as backup.

## Usage Notes

- Use the pack to make decisions, not to admire a dashboard until everyone’s calendar loses the will to live.
- Include thresholds and trends so the review can distinguish noise from deterioration.
- Carry forward actions with owners and dates; unresolved action sludge is a process smell.

## Example

A monthly support-operations review might inspect ticket volume, queue age, SLA breaches, escalation themes, customer sentiment, staffing gaps, knowledge-base defects, and two decisions: whether to adjust routing rules and whether to fund automation for repetitive password-reset failures.

# Compliance Obligation Inventory

## Purpose

Track legal, regulatory, contractual, policy, standards, customer, certification, insurance, grant, or board obligations in plain language with ownership, controls, evidence, review cadence, and change monitoring.

## Usage Notes

- Keep obligation summaries understandable by operating owners, not only specialists who enjoy abbreviations with regulatory aftertaste.
- Link obligations to real controls and evidence. An inventory without operating links is just a compliance scrapbook.
- Review after regulatory changes, new products, new jurisdictions, major customer commitments, audit findings, or contract changes.

## Example

A SaaS vendor might track SOC 2 commitments, customer data-processing terms, privacy notice obligations, breach notification timelines, accessibility commitments, security review evidence, and regional data-retention requirements.

## Usage Notes

- Exceptions should expire. Permanent exceptions are policy changes hiding under a blanket.
- Match approval authority to risk and obligation type; do not let convenience accept risk above its pay grade.
- Review recurring exceptions as process-design evidence, not as individual moral failure.

## Example

A temporary waiver from a standard vendor review may be approved for a low-risk emergency supplier if data exposure is limited, contract terms are constrained, compensating monitoring is assigned, and full review is due before renewal.

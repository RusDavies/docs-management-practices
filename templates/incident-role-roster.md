## Usage Notes

- Small teams may combine roles, but the combined owner must still know which hat they are wearing.
- Update the roster when severity changes, response spans shifts, or external/legal/customer handling begins.
- Pair with the incident command runbook and timeline/decision log.

## Example

During a high-severity outage, the engineering manager may act as incident commander while a senior engineer leads recovery, support owns customer-impact collection, and legal/security join only if data exposure becomes plausible.

## Usage Notes

- Rituals should make useful behaviour easier, not merely fill calendar real estate.
- Retire rituals that exist only because nobody wants to offend the ghost of an old process.
- Review during growth, leadership change, remote/hybrid redesign, or repeated meeting fatigue.

## Example

A weekly “hero awards” ritual may unintentionally reward firefighting over prevention. Change it to recognize prevention, simplification, customer recovery, and quiet reliability as well as urgent rescue.

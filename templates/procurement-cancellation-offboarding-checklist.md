## Usage Notes

- Start this before contract end when the supplier supports a business process, data flow, customer commitment, or system integration.
- Offboarding is not finished when invoices stop. Access, data, evidence, and operational ownership also need closure.
- Pair with the renewal calendar and supplier risk questionnaire.

## Example

When cancelling a marketing automation platform, the checklist should cover notice, stakeholder communications, data export, integration shutdown, access removal, final billing, evidence archival, and updates to customer-facing workflows.

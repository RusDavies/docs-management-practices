# Operations Launch / Readiness Checklist

## Purpose

Check whether a new or changed service, product, workflow, vendor dependency, or operating model is ready to run, support, monitor, recover, and improve after launch.

## Usage Notes

- Passing a build, publishing a policy, or signing a vendor contract does not mean operations are ready. Reality remains rudely multi-disciplinary.
- Do not bury major readiness gaps as “post-launch follow-up” unless someone accountable accepts the risk.
- Reuse with change-management go/no-go checks when the launch materially changes people’s work.

## Example

A new customer portal launch might require support scripts, monitoring, account-provisioning SOPs, rollback steps, customer communications, vendor escalation contacts, access reviews, service thresholds, and a two-week stabilization review cadence.

## Usage Notes

- Appoint one customer communication owner.
- Track every new commitment made during escalation.
- Separate immediate containment from root-cause remediation.
- Close the escalation only when commitments, communication, and recovery review are complete.

## Example

For a failed customer launch, record the customer impact, recovery owner, revised launch actions, update cadence, executive sponsor, and commitments made during recovery.

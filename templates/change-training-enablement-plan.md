## Usage Notes

- Train close enough to use. Training three months early is a memory-leak exercise with snacks.
- Separate awareness from ability. A person can know a process exists and still be completely unready to use it under pressure.
- Update knowledge bases, support scripts, runbooks, and manager materials before launch, not after the first wave of confusion files a ticket.

## Example

For a new incident-management process, enablement might include incident commander walkthroughs, escalation matrix job aids, support scripts, manager talking points, scenario drills, and post-launch office hours.

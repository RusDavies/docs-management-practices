## Usage Notes

- Make the official intake path easier than back-channel pleading, or the back channel will become the real process with worse lighting.
- Define return criteria kindly and clearly; bad intake data creates downstream operational tax.
- Revisit rules when queue aging, escalations, duplicate requests, or side-channel work increase.

## Example

A legal review queue might require contract type, counterparty, deadline driver, risk flags, commercial owner, and source document. Requests missing the contract or deadline rationale return for clarification rather than entering invisible limbo.

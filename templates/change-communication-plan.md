## Usage Notes

- Say what is unknown. Fake certainty is how trust goes to die in a branded slide deck.
- Match the sender to the message. Sponsors explain why; managers explain local implications; support explains where help lives.
- Pair communication with engagement: questions, feedback, walkthroughs, office hours, and visible action on legitimate concerns.

## Example

For a new approval workflow, executives may announce the business reason, managers may explain team-specific impacts, procurement may run walkthroughs, and support may publish how to request help or exceptions.

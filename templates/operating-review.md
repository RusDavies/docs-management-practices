## Usage Notes

- Use for periodic review of outcomes, priorities, metrics, risks, decisions, and commitments.
- Focus on evidence and decisions, not performative status recitation.
- Carry unresolved decisions and commitments forward until closed or deliberately dropped.
- Use the review to adjust the operating model when patterns repeat.

## Example

A monthly product operating review might cover adoption, customer issues, roadmap tradeoffs, operational risks, decisions needed on scope, and commitments for the next period.

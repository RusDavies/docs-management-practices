# Risk Review Agenda

## Purpose

Run a risk review that focuses on changes, decisions, ownership, mitigation progress, acceptances, escalations, and learning rather than reading a register aloud like a bedtime story for auditors.

## Usage Notes

- Ask what changed, what needs a decision, and what evidence is missing. Do not merely admire the same top-ten risks every month.
- Bring owners who can act or escalate. Spectator-heavy risk reviews are expensive weather reports.
- Close the review with named actions, dates, and escalation owners.

## Example

A monthly portfolio risk review might focus on one new vendor-concentration risk, two overdue mitigations, one accepted risk nearing expiry, and a decision to either fund additional delivery capacity or reduce launch scope.

## Usage Notes

- Use for Scrum or iteration-based agile teams, especially when predictability, focus, or learning is degrading.
- Review whether feedback changed decisions. If nothing can change, stop pretending the ceremony is agile and call it what it is: staged execution in a cardigan.
- Do not turn the review into blame for missed estimates when leadership changed the inputs mid-sprint.

## Example

A team misses its sprint goal because urgent production defects consumed half the capacity. The health review should decide whether to reserve capacity, split urgent flow into a Kanban lane, fix the reliability source, adjust sprint planning, or change the delivery model.

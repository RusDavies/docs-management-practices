# Compliance Regulatory-Change Log

## Purpose

Track regulatory, legal, contractual, standards, customer, or policy changes from discovery through triage, impact analysis, decision, implementation, communication, and evidence update.

## Usage Notes

- Record sources and citations. “I saw a LinkedIn post about this” is not regulatory intelligence, adorable though it may be.
- Route legal interpretation to qualified reviewers; managers should not freestyle formal legal positions.
- Connect changes to products, processes, controls, vendors, data, training, and customer commitments.

## Example

A new privacy-rule deadline may trigger product consent-flow review, vendor data-processing updates, retention-policy changes, customer notice updates, training changes, and audit evidence updates.

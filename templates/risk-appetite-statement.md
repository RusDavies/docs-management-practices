# Risk Appetite Statement

## Purpose

Define the amount and type of risk the organization, portfolio, product, function, or team is willing to take in pursuit of its objectives, and where escalation is required.

## Scope

- Organization / portfolio / function / team:
- Statement owner:
- Approving authority:

## Review Triggers

- Major incident, loss, audit finding, or near miss:
- New regulation, contract, or obligation:
- Significant product / market / customer change:
- Major vendor, platform, or architecture change:
- Reorganization or capability change:

## Usage Notes

- Use appetite to guide real decisions, not to create a laminated statement of executive vibes.
- Define escalation triggers in plain language so teams know when they are outside their authority.
- Review appetite when evidence changes; stale appetite is just old confidence wearing a suit.

## Example

A payments product may have low appetite for customer-funds accuracy, privacy exposure, and regulatory breaches, moderate appetite for controlled product experiments, and higher appetite for reversible UX tests with clear rollback and no sensitive-data exposure.

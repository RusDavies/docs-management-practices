## Usage Notes

- Review evidence patterns, not anecdotes in isolation.
- Keep survey scores in context; one number is a clue, not an oracle wearing a lanyard.
- Track whether actions are visible to the people affected.

## Example

A team may report strong belonging but weak dissent. Record the mixed signal, inspect meeting and decision norms, and create an action for leaders to invite counterarguments before major decisions.

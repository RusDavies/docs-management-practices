## Usage Notes

- Review risk early enough that the organization can act without panic discounts, roadmap bribery, or executive theatre.
- Separate usage health from trust health. High usage with angry executives is not green; it is churn wearing a productivity hat.
- Pair with customer health review, commitment register, escalation plan, and feedback synthesis log.

## Example

A customer with strong adoption but repeated unresolved support escalations may need an executive recovery conversation, support fix plan, product issue owner, and weekly progress updates before renewal negotiation begins.

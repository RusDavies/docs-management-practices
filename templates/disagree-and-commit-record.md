## Usage Notes

- Use when disagreement is legitimate, material, and should be preserved without blocking execution forever.
- Summarize objections fairly; do not caricature the losing side.
- Record accepted risks and the decision owner.
- Reopen only if new material evidence appears or the review trigger is met.

## Example

Engineering disagrees with launching before a performance fix; Product recommends launch due to customer deadline. Record the risk, mitigation, final launch decision, expected commitment, and review date after monitoring data is available.

## Evidence Questions

- Who gets invited into important conversations?
- Who receives stretch assignments, sponsorship, and useful feedback?
- Who is interrupted, ignored, over-scrutinized, or asked to do invisible work?
- Are similar behaviours handled consistently across status, tenure, identity, location, or function?
- Are examples, stories, and recognition patterns reinforcing narrow assumptions about competence?
- Where do informal networks decide access before formal processes begin?

## Usage Notes

- Focus on observable systems and behaviours; avoid turning people into demographic props.
- Use role-only examples when named personas would distract or create avoidable baggage.
- Escalate legal, harassment, discrimination, or employee-relations concerns through the appropriate channels.

## Example

If the same small group receives most high-visibility recovery work, review whether assignment practices are fair, whether hidden sponsorship is operating, and whether recognition tells a narrow story about who “saves” the business.

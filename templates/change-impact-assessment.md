## Usage Notes

- Include people close to the work. Impact assessment done only by senior sponsors is usually a map of the building drawn from orbit.
- Severity should reflect operational disruption, customer risk, compliance/control impact, and human workload.
- Feed high-impact items directly into readiness, communication, training, rollout, and support plans.

## Example

A CRM migration may affect sales pipeline stages, customer-success renewal views, finance forecasting, support account context, executive dashboards, and integrations. Treating it as only a sales-tool change guarantees a festive little failure parade.

## Usage Notes

- Use for critical responsibilities, not only formal leadership roles.
- Record readiness evidence, not just opinions.
- Treat emergency backup as risk reduction, not succession solved.
- Keep confidentiality explicit; succession notes can affect trust, careers, and retention.

## Example

For a technical lead who is the only person able to approve high-risk production changes, record the current owner, backup engineer, required access, design/release knowledge gaps, development actions, and a review date for readiness.

## Usage Notes

- On-call health is an engineering-management issue, not a heroic suffering contest.
- Use product operations and observability guidance for detailed monitoring, alerting, runbook, and incident evidence expectations.
- Treat recurring pages, stale runbooks, and unresolved corrective actions as system-design feedback.

## Example

A team with frequent after-hours database alerts may decide to tune alert thresholds, add self-healing for safe restarts, improve dashboard context, rotate secondary coverage, and fund the reliability fix instead of applauding everyone’s exhaustion.

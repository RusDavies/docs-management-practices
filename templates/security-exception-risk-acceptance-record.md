## Usage Notes

- Exceptions should be rare, visible, owned, and time-bound. Invisible exceptions are just security debt wearing camouflage.
- Approval authority must match exposure. Convenience should not accept enterprise risk on a Tuesday because the release train is impatient.
- Renewals should trigger scrutiny; repeated renewal may mean the standard, architecture, funding, or risk appetite needs a real decision.

## Example

A high-priority customer launch may accept a low-reachability dependency finding for 30 days if the vulnerable path is not exposed, monitoring is added, exploitability evidence is documented, and upgrade work is scheduled before the exception expires.

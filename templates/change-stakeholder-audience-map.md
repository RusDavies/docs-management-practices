## Usage Notes

- Do not label resistance as attitude by default. Resistance may be the first honest bug report from the operating model.
- Separate people who approve the change from people who must live with it. Those are often different mammals.
- Keep this map current through rollout; stakeholder stance changes as details become real.

## Example

For a new support triage model, stakeholders may include frontline support agents, support managers, engineering escalation owners, customer-success teams, customers with active incidents, finance if SLAs affect credits, and executives who must stop bypassing the queue.

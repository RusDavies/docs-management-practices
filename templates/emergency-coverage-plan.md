## Usage Notes

- Use this as a bridge, not as a permanent succession plan.
- Test access and authority before the owner is unavailable.
- Pair with a succession risk register when the gap is material.

## Example

For an account owner out unexpectedly, identify the backup relationship owner, customer contacts, open commitments, escalation history, contract dates, and decisions that require executive approval.

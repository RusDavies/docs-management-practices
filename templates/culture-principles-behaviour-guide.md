## Usage Notes

- Use behaviour words, not poster words.
- Include anti-examples so people know where the boundary is.
- Review after major growth, reorganization, crisis, acquisition, leadership change, or repeated cultural friction.
- Do not publish principles that leaders are unwilling to be judged against. People notice. Annoyingly observant species.

## Example

Principle: “Disagree openly, commit honestly.”

This means leaders invite dissent before decisions, name tradeoffs, document the decision, and stop relitigating after commitment unless new evidence appears. It does not mean consensus, passive compliance, or punishing someone for raising risk before the decision.

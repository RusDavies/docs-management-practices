## Usage Notes

- A handoff is not complete because someone sent a message. It is complete when the receiving owner accepts usable work.
- Put handoffs in the system of record where possible. Chat is useful; memory is not a database, despite humanity’s brave experiments.
- Review after recurring delays, rework, escalations, or “I thought they had it” incidents.

## Example

A product-to-support launch handoff might require release notes, known issues, support scripts, escalation contacts, customer-impact notes, monitoring links, and explicit support acceptance before go-live.

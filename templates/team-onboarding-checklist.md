## Usage Notes

- Onboarding is not access provisioning with a welcome GIF stapled to it.
- Include decision history and unwritten norms before the new person learns them through avoidable confusion.
- Review the checklist with the new person; onboarding should adapt to role, experience, and context.

## Example

A new team lead may need system access, team charter, working agreement, stakeholder map, current risks, key one-on-one context, escalation paths, delivery cadence, and a first-month goal to improve one recurring team friction.

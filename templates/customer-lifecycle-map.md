# Customer Lifecycle Map

## Purpose

Map the customer journey from qualification through onboarding, adoption, support, renewal, expansion, escalation, recovery, and exit. Use this to make ownership, handoffs, commitments, and customer experience visible across functions.

## Usage Notes

- Use this when ownership is unclear, handoffs are weak, onboarding or renewal risk is recurring, or customer experience differs wildly by function.
- Keep the map honest: if customers experience five teams as one confused blob, the lifecycle map should expose that rather than decorating it.
- Pair with the customer commitment register, customer health review, onboarding plan, renewal risk review, and offboarding checklist.

## Example

A B2B SaaS lifecycle map may show that sales owns qualification and contract context, implementation owns onboarding milestones, customer success owns adoption and renewal health, support owns issue resolution, product owns feedback synthesis, and account management owns commercial renewal and expansion.

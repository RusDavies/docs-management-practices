## Usage Notes

- Use for decisions that need memory beyond the meeting.
- Keep it concise: context, options, disagreement, decision, commitment, and review trigger.
- Record meaningful objections before the decision so they do not reappear later as folklore.
- Share the final record with affected stakeholders.

## Example

Decision: use a phased rollout rather than big-bang launch. Record the customer-risk rationale, rejected option, operational concern raised by Support, decision owner, commitment expected, and review trigger after pilot completion.

## Closure Criteria

- Completion evidence reviewed:
- Residual risk accepted, if any:
- Risk register updated, if needed:
- Related runbooks or controls updated:
- Lessons shared with relevant teams:
- Tracker closed by:
- Closure date:

## Usage Notes

- Every accepted action needs an owner, due date, and evidence expectation.
- Do not assign fifteen vague improvements when three concrete fixes would actually happen.
- Pair with the post-incident review, risk register, and operational review cadence.

## Example

After an outage caused by a missing deployment rollback check, corrective actions might include adding a rollback test, updating the deployment runbook, changing release approval gates, and reviewing the change after the next two deployments.

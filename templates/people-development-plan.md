# People Development Plan

Use this template to create a practical development plan for an individual contributor, manager, internal transfer, promotion candidate, or person rebuilding capability after a role change.

A development plan is not a horoscope with quarterly milestones. It should connect real capability gaps to real work, support, feedback, and evidence.

## 30 / 60 / 90-Day Reviews

### 30-Day Review

- What progress is visible?
- What support was promised and actually provided?
- What is still unclear?
- What should change in the plan?
- Are the goals still realistic and useful?

### 60-Day Review

- Which behaviours or outputs have improved?
- What evidence is still missing?
- Are any blockers outside the person's control?
- Does the plan need more authority, exposure, coaching, or workload adjustment?

### 90-Day Review

- Which goals are complete?
- Which goals should continue?
- What capability is now demonstrated by evidence?
- What decision follows: continue plan, close plan, change role expectations, consider promotion/readiness path, or escalate a performance/support concern?

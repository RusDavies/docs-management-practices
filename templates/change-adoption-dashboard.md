## Usage Notes

- Measure behaviour and outcomes, not just emails sent and training attended. Activity metrics are not adoption; they are confetti with numbers.
- Track old-process usage explicitly. A hidden workaround is a dashboard entry begging to be noticed.
- Use feedback themes to improve the rollout, not to rank who complained most poetically.

## Example

For a new procurement approval process, adoption signals could include percentage of requests submitted through the tool, approval cycle time, exception volume, policy violations, help tickets, and reduction in off-system purchases.

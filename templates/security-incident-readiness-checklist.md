# Security Incident Readiness Checklist

## Purpose

Check whether the organization, product, service, or team is prepared to detect, escalate, investigate, contain, communicate, recover from, and learn from a security incident.

## Usage Notes

- Run this before reality schedules the tabletop for you.
- Connect readiness gaps to owners and dates; “we should improve logging” is not readiness, it is a wish with anxiety.
- Re-check after major launches, architecture changes, vendor changes, incidents, or new sensitive-data flows.

## Example

A customer-facing SaaS product may discover that credential rotation is ready but customer notification routing is unclear. That gap should be assigned before the next incident, not during the first hour of one.

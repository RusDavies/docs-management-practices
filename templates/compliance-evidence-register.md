# Compliance Evidence Register

## Purpose

Track required compliance evidence, where it lives, who owns it, retention expectations, access restrictions, and whether it is current enough to support audits, reviews, customer requests, or internal assurance.

## Usage Notes

- Strong evidence is specific, dated, attributable, and tied to a control or obligation.
- Avoid folders named `audit stuff final FINAL v3`. That way lies madness, or at least a very avoidable Thursday.
- Treat sensitive, privileged, personal, customer, or confidential evidence with appropriate access controls.

## Example

Evidence for vendor-security review might include completed questionnaires, SOC 2 reports, issue remediation records, approval notes, renewal decisions, and proof of data-processing terms, each with owner and retention expectations.

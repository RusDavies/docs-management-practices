## Usage Notes

- Keep the agreement short enough to remember and real enough to use during actual work.
- Revisit when the team changes size, scope, location pattern, operating cadence, or frustration level.
- If nobody can point to the agreement during a disagreement, it is probably decorative stationery.

## Example

A hybrid product team may agree that decisions live in written records, urgent production issues can interrupt focus time, roadmap debates happen in planning forums rather than side chats, and meeting notes must include owners and dates.

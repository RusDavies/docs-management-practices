# Exception Register

## Purpose

Track operational exceptions deliberately: what was allowed, why, who approved it, what compensating controls apply, when it expires, and whether recurring exceptions indicate the standard process needs redesign.

## Usage Notes

- Exceptions need owners, expiry, and review. Permanent exceptions are usually the real process trying to escape its paperwork cage.
- Include compensating controls when risk, compliance, security, customer, financial, or operational exposure changes.
- Use recurring exceptions as improvement evidence; either fix the process or admit the rule is wrong.

## Example

A manual billing adjustment outside the standard workflow might require finance approval, evidence retention, customer-success notification, reconciliation review, expiry after one billing cycle, and follow-up if similar cases repeat.

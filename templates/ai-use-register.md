# AI Use Register

Use this register to track material AI assistance in drafting, review, synthesis, repository maintenance, publication preparation, decision support, or agentic work.

The point is accountability, not decorative guilt. If a later reviewer needs to know what the machine helped with, record enough that future-you does not have to perform archaeology with a head injury.

## Review Checklist

- [ ] Entry exists for material AI assistance.
- [ ] Tool/provider was appropriate for the data class.
- [ ] Human owner is named.
- [ ] Inputs and outputs are described without leaking sensitive content.
- [ ] Factual claims and citations were checked.
- [ ] Sensitive examples were reviewed for privacy and publication risk.
- [ ] People-impacting or regulated-domain material received appropriate review.
- [ ] Disclosure decision was recorded.
- [ ] Correction owner/path is clear.

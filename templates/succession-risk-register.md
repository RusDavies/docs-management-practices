## Coverage Levels

1. No coverage identified — known risk; must be accepted or addressed.
2. Emergency backup — someone can keep the lights on temporarily.
3. Ready soon — someone could step in with support after a short transition.
4. Ready now — someone can assume the role/responsibility with minimal disruption.
5. Redundant capability — multiple capable owners; no meaningful single point of failure.

## Usage Notes

- Use for critical responsibilities, not only formal job titles.
- Review roles where one person holds operational knowledge, access, customer trust, or decision authority.
- Treat “emergency backup” as risk reduction, not succession solved.
- Link development actions to real readiness gaps.

## Example

For a sole database administrator, record critical responsibilities, backup coverage, access/documentation gaps, potential deputy, development actions, and accepted residual risk if coverage remains weak.

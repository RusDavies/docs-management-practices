## Usage Notes

- Keep the policy light enough that people will follow it.
- High-risk content needs stronger review than local notes.
- Archive stale content that must be preserved; do not leave it wearing an “approved” hat and causing little documentation crimes.

## Example

A runbook becomes review-required after a tooling change, incident, or service ownership change. If replaced, it is marked superseded with a link to the new runbook and excluded from AI retrieval.

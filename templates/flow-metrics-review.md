## Usage Notes

- Use for Kanban, support queues, platform teams, maintenance streams, and any delivery model where flow matters.
- Review aging and blocked work before celebrating throughput; otherwise the dashboard just rewards easy work while risk fossilizes in a corner.
- Do not weaponize individual metrics. Use flow metrics to improve the system, not to rank people by ticket confetti.

## Example

A platform team may discover that urgent access requests repeatedly jump the queue, dependency updates age beyond policy, and review bottlenecks sit with one overloaded specialist. The review should change intake, WIP, review ownership, or escalation policy.

## Usage Notes

- Offboarding should be respectful even when the customer was a bad fit. Petty exits are not a strategy; they are brand damage with admin overhead.
- Confirm data, access, contract, billing, and support closure with evidence. Lingering access after churn is not “customer-friendly”; it is a control failure.
- Pair with churn/loss review when the exit reveals product, support, sales, relationship, or strategy lessons.

## Example

A customer leaving for a competitor may need data export, admin access closure, final invoice reconciliation, support entitlement shutdown, integration key revocation, CRM update, and a churn review to determine whether the loss reflects product gap, pricing, or unresolved trust damage.

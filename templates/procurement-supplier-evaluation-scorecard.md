## Usage Notes

- Scorecards should explain the decision, not decorate it after the preferred supplier has already won by charisma.
- Do not average away fatal concerns. A supplier can score well overall and still fail on privacy, contract terms, or operational support.
- Pair with the supplier risk questionnaire, purchase request, and approval threshold matrix.

## Example

Three help-desk platforms may all meet core ticketing needs, but one may require less migration effort, one may have stronger reporting, and one may create unacceptable data-residency risk. The scorecard should make that trade-off visible.

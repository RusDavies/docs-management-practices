## Method Indicators

| Method | Strong indicators | Warning signs | Fit for this work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Waterfall / sequential | Requirements stable, change costly, phases/gates required | Major discovery remains, feedback should change scope |  |
| Agile / iterative | Uncertainty, frequent feedback, adaptable scope | Fixed scope/budget/schedule pretending to be flexible |  |
| Scrum | Stable cross-functional team, sprint goals, active product owner | Heavy interrupts, absent decision owner, ceremonies only |  |
| Kanban / flow | Continuous intake, variable urgency, WIP/cycle-time concerns | Need for coherent sprint goals or large planned increments |  |
| Hybrid / stage-gated agile | Governance plus learning loops | Paperwork waterfall with agile vocabulary stapled on |  |

## Usage Notes

- This checklist supports judgment; it does not replace it. A spreadsheet cannot save humans from lying to themselves, tragic though that is.
- Use when starting a major initiative, rescuing a struggling project, changing delivery model, or explaining methodology tradeoffs to stakeholders.
- Pair with a delivery approach decision record when the choice affects commitments, funding, governance, or delivery risk.

## Example

A support-operations backlog with unpredictable urgent requests, maintenance tasks, and aging blocked work will likely score toward Kanban. A new product feature with active user discovery and a stable team may score toward Scrum or broader agile delivery.

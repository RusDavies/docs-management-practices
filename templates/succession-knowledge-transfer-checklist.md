# Succession Knowledge-Transfer Checklist

## Purpose

Capture and transfer the knowledge, access, relationships, routines, decision history, and context needed for continuity when a critical role or responsibility changes hands.

Use this for planned transitions, emergency coverage preparation, successor development, founder dependency reduction, specialist handoff, customer/account transition, or operational backup creation.

## Usage Notes

- Knowledge transfer is not complete because a folder exists. Verify that the receiver can use the knowledge under realistic conditions.
- Separate sensitive access from general documentation. Do not dump secrets, HR details, financial authority, or private customer context into broad notes because “handoff” sounded noble.
- Use alongside successor development plans, emergency coverage plans, succession risk registers, and knowledge-management source-of-truth registers.

## Example

For a customer-account owner transition, the checklist may include account history, relationship sensitivities, renewal commitments, escalation history, contract dates, product promises, stakeholder introductions, CRM updates, and a validation call where the backup owner leads the next customer review.

# Content Ownership Register

## Purpose

Track who owns important documents, knowledge bases, runbooks, policies, templates, articles, and registers.

## Scope

- Repository / workspace / knowledge base:
- Register owner:
- Review date:

## Owner Responsibilities

For each owner, confirm responsibility for:

- accuracy
- approval status
- freshness and review triggers
- access and sensitivity classification
- duplicate/conflicting source resolution
- archive or deprecation decisions
- feedback and gap handling
- AI retrieval eligibility where relevant

## Usage Notes

- Review ownership after reorganizations, role changes, tool migrations, product launches, incidents, and audit findings.
- Do not let critical content be owned by someone who lacks authority to keep it true.
- Backup ownership matters for continuity. Otherwise the wiki has a bus factor, because apparently even pages can be fragile.

## Example

A production runbook may be owned by the service team lead, backed up by the on-call lead, reviewed after every major incident, and restricted to internal engineering and operations audiences.

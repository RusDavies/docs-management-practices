## Representation and Example Optics

- [ ] Positive, competent, leadership, recovery, and good-judgment examples intentionally include women and culturally diverse personas where named examples are used.
- [ ] Negative, failure, dysfunction, risk, and anti-pattern examples do not make women or diverse personas carry the problem case.
- [ ] White male-coded names are not the default hero, beneficiary, rescuer, or assumed competent actor.
- [ ] Named examples do not create a simplistic identity-to-virtue or identity-to-fault pattern.
- [ ] Role-only examples are used where a named persona would distract or create avoidable demographic baggage.
- [ ] The full document has been reviewed for aggregate patterns, not just individual examples.

## Sensitive Topic Gate

For DEI, equity, candidate-pool asymmetry, demographics, hiring-stage correction, protected characteristics, politics, legal/regulatory claims, or other sensitive content:

- [ ] No sensitive section present.
- [ ] Sensitive section present and reviewed with `PUBLICATION_DECISION_GATES.md`.
- [ ] Decision: publish as written.
- [ ] Decision: publish after revision.
- [ ] Decision: exclude from public version.
- [ ] Decision: replace with safer framing.

## Brand / Employment Risk

- [ ] The document cannot easily be clipped into an unintended anti-DEI, hostile, resentful, or discriminatory position.
- [ ] The document sounds constructive and professionally useful.
- [ ] The document avoids needless culture-war framing.
- [ ] Claims are proportionate, sourced where needed, and not overstated.

## Practical Quality

- [ ] The document has a clear audience.
- [ ] The document makes management responsibilities clearer.
- [ ] The document includes useful examples or artifacts.
- [ ] The document avoids sterile role-label mush where human examples would improve readability.
- [ ] The document avoids jargon theatre.

## Usage Notes

- Use before public, customer-facing, executive, or employment-facing publication.
- Review the full document pattern, not only individual examples.
- Use the sensitive-topic gate when protected traits, hiring equity, politics, legal/regulatory claims, or culture-war-adjacent material appears.
- Record required changes before approval.

## Example

Before publishing a people-management guide, check whether positive examples include varied personas, negative examples avoid stereotype-loading, sensitive hiring/equity sections are gated, and the document cannot easily be clipped into a hostile framing.

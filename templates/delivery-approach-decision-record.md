# Delivery Approach Decision Record

## Purpose

Record why a team chose waterfall, agile, Scrum, Kanban, hybrid, staged, or another delivery approach for a specific body of work. Use this when the choice affects commitments, governance, stakeholder expectations, funding, risk, or team operating cadence.

## Usage Notes

- Use this before everyone starts performing agile interpretive dance around fixed commitments.
- Be explicit about fixed constraints and negotiable constraints; hidden constraints become blame machines later.
- Re-open the decision when assumptions break, interruption load changes, governance expands, or feedback proves the method is mismatched.

## Example

A regulatory remediation project with known control gaps, a fixed audit date, and defined evidence requirements may choose staged delivery with agile execution inside phases. The decision record should say which gates are mandatory, which backlog items can change, and who owns tradeoff decisions.

## Usage Notes

- Use this when the decision affects strategic differentiation, cost, security, vendor lock-in, maintenance burden, open-source obligations, or operational ownership.
- Buying does not remove ownership; it changes the shape of ownership into vendor, contract, integration, data, and exit-path management. Tedious, but true.
- Link to `docs-software-product-process` procurement, architecture, security, compliance, and operations guidance where the decision affects a software product.

## Example

A team deciding whether to build an audit-log system may compare internal build effort, existing platform reuse, and a vendor product against retention, integrity, export, customer trust, SOC 2 evidence, operations load, cost, and long-term exit path.

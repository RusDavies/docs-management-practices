## Usage Notes

- Use when content was once authoritative or likely to be rediscovered.
- Always link the replacement when one exists.
- Exclude deprecated content from AI retrieval unless there is a deliberate historical-use case with clear labels.

## Example

A retired onboarding guide is archived for historical reference, removed from new-hire navigation, excluded from AI retrieval, and replaced by a role-specific onboarding knowledge pack.

# Customer Churn / Loss Review

## Purpose

Review why a customer left, failed to renew, downgraded materially, or was intentionally exited. Use this to identify avoidable causes, product/market-fit signals, support or trust failures, sales qualification gaps, and strategic choices.

## Usage Notes

- Use this for churn, non-renewal, major downgrade, failed onboarding, competitive loss, or deliberate customer exit.
- Be honest. “Budget” may be the customer’s polite explanation for months of support pain. Humans do this. Annoying, but documented.
- Aggregate repeated reviews into product, sales, customer success, and operations planning.

## Example

A customer churns citing cost, but the review shows repeated implementation delays, unresolved support escalations, and weak sponsor engagement. The action plan should not simply say “discount earlier next time.” That would be impressively missing the point.

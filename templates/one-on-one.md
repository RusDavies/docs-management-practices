## Usage Notes

- Use for recurring manager/employee conversations, not status interrogation.
- Track commitments and follow-up over time.
- Include feedback, coaching, blockers, development, and wellbeing where appropriate.
- Keep sensitive notes respectful and access-controlled.

## Example

A useful 1:1 note might capture a blocker with another team, feedback on meeting facilitation, a development goal, and a manager commitment to clarify priorities by Friday.

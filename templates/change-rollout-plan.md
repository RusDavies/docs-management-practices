## Usage Notes

- Name the old process retirement plan. If the old way remains easier, humans will follow the path of least bureaucratic suffering.
- Use pilots to learn, not to rubber-stamp a decision that has already become sacred.
- Define pause triggers before launch pressure starts turning every warning into “manageable noise.”
- For production rollouts, distinguish the change lead from the hands-on implementer, reviewer/verifier, rollback authority, and support handoff owner.

## Example

A new ticket triage process might pilot with one support queue, expand by product area, run old/new routing in parallel for one week, publish escalation paths, and retire direct engineering pings after support coverage proves stable.

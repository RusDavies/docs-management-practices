## Usage Notes

- Readiness is evidenced, not wished into existence by calendar pressure.
- Use “go with conditions” only when conditions are explicit, owned, and tracked. Otherwise it is just “go” wearing a safety vest.
- Reassess after major scope, date, system, support, or stakeholder changes.

## Example

A policy rollout may be communication-ready but not manager-ready if team leads cannot explain exception handling. Record that as a condition, not as a vague “managers to cascade message” incantation.

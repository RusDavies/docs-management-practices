## Usage Notes

- Put ownership where the real work happens. Security can coordinate, challenge, and advise; it cannot magically operate every control by staring intensely at a spreadsheet.
- Record failure conditions so control owners know when to escalate.
- Review after incidents, audit findings, system ownership changes, reorgs, or repeated exceptions.

## Example

A production-access control may be accountable to the platform owner, operated by identity administration, reviewed by system owners quarterly, evidenced by access-review exports and revocation records, and escalated when owners fail to certify access on time.

# Role / Responsibility Map

## Purpose

Clarify who owns important responsibilities, decisions, backups, handoffs, and collaboration points so work does not disappear into the classic fog bank of “I thought someone else had that.”

## Usage Notes

- Use this for responsibilities that matter enough to create dropped work, duplicated work, dependency delays, or political fog.
- Name backups for critical responsibilities. One heroic expert is not a resilience strategy; it is a future outage with a LinkedIn profile.
- Review after staffing changes, reorgs, scope changes, incidents, or repeated “who owns this?” meetings.

## Example

An engineering team might map service ownership, release approval, incident commander rotation, product discovery input, code-review standards, security triage, vendor escalation, and customer-facing technical explanation ownership.

## Usage Notes

- Use when expectations need to be made explicit for support, coaching, performance repair, or role clarity.
- Describe outcomes and behaviours separately.
- Include support the manager/organization will provide.
- Make milestones, review date, and consequences clear but proportionate.

## Example

For a manager missing delivery follow-through, define expected planning cadence, stakeholder updates, risk escalation, support/coaching, milestone dates, and what happens if reliability does not improve.

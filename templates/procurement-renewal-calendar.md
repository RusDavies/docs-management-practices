## Usage Notes

- Start renewal review before the notice deadline, not after it has quietly passed and joined the list of things everyone pretends were unavoidable.
- Include owner names. A renewal without an owner is a subscription with a hostage note.
- Pair with the supplier evaluation scorecard and cancellation/offboarding checklist.

## Example

A critical data-platform renewal might start review 120 days before notice, compare cost and usage, verify security evidence, test exit feasibility, and require executive approval before renewal.

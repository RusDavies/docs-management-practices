## Usage Notes

- Ask only questions that someone will actually review. Questionnaire theatre is still theatre, just with more cells.
- Tailor depth to criticality, data exposure, and dependency. A strategic platform needs more evidence than office snacks.
- Reuse at renewal when scope, data exposure, incidents, ownership, or contract terms have changed.

## Example

A payroll supplier review would require data residency, access control, incident notification, subprocessor, retention, continuity, and regulatory evidence before approval or renewal.

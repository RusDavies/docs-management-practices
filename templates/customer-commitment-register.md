## Usage Notes

- Use this whenever customer promises could affect roadmap, delivery, support, pricing, compliance, or executive expectations.
- Record the source of the commitment: contract, email, meeting, sales note, support case, or executive conversation.
- Review open commitments before roadmap, renewal, launch, and escalation discussions.
- Close commitments only with evidence and customer notification where appropriate.

## Example

If Sales promises a customer a custom report by quarter-end, record the customer, source, owner, due date, decision authority, delivery risk, and whether Product/Engineering/Support accepted the commitment.

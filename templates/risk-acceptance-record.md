## Usage Notes

- Accepted risk is not ignored risk. It is a decision with a timer and a name attached.
- Do not use acceptance to avoid uncomfortable mitigation conversations when the risk exceeds appetite.
- Reopen acceptance when assumptions, controls, incidents, customer commitments, or regulatory obligations change.

## Example

A team may accept temporary manual reconciliation for one reporting cycle if automated reconciliation is not ready, compensating review is assigned, financial exposure is bounded, and acceptance expires before the next close.

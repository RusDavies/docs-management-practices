## Usage Notes

- Approval authority should follow both spend and risk. Cheap access to sensitive systems is not magically low risk because the invoice is tiny.
- Publish the matrix where managers can find it before they improvise their own purchasing folklore.
- Review thresholds periodically against spend patterns, audit findings, incidents, and supplier-risk trends.

## Example

A $2,000 design subscription may need only team and budget-owner approval, while a $2,000 AI tool processing customer data may require procurement, security, privacy, and legal review because the risk is not the invoice.

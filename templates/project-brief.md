## Usage Notes

- Use before significant work begins or when a messy initiative needs re-framing.
- Keep the brief focused on outcome, scope, constraints, decision owner, risks, and approach.
- Revisit the brief when scope, schedule, sponsor, or constraints change.
- Use it to align stakeholders before detailed planning.

## Example

For a customer-portal project, define the customer problem, desired outcomes, non-goals, stakeholders, delivery approach, scope boundaries, constraints, risks, milestones, and communication cadence.

## Escalation Rules

Escalate when:

- likelihood or impact becomes high
- mitigation is blocked
- acceptance requires authority above the current owner
- risk affects customer trust, safety, legal/compliance posture, security, finance, or public reputation

## Usage Notes

- Use for risks that need ownership, review, and response decisions.
- Make likelihood/impact scales meaningful for the context.
- Review accepted risks; acceptance is not a magic bin where concern goes to die.
- Escalate when the current owner cannot accept or mitigate the risk.

## Example

For a vendor dependency risk, record likelihood, impact, owner, mitigation, fallback/exit plan, review date, and whether acceptance requires executive or procurement approval.

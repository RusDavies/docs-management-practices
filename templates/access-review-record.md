## Usage Notes

- Reviewers must understand the roles they certify. Rubber-stamping unknown permissions is access governance by vibes.
- Include service accounts, machine identities, vendors, and agents where they can access meaningful systems or data.
- Track revocation evidence; “remove later” has a suspicious habit of becoming archaeology.

## Example

A quarterly production-access review might certify engineers with break-glass access, remove departed vendor accounts, expire temporary deployment roles, and flag unclear admin groups for role redesign.

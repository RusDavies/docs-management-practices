## Usage Notes

- Do not reduce team health to one survey score. A cheerful team can still be quietly on fire.
- Look for patterns, not just dramatic incidents.
- Pick a small number of follow-up actions and actually complete them. Otherwise the review becomes emotional archaeology.

## Example

A team may show strong trust and quality but poor workload health because support interruptions consume planned work. The review should trigger an interruption policy, capacity discussion, or support handoff fix, not a pep talk.

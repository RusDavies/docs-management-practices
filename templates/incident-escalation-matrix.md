## Usage Notes

- Escalation should add authority, expertise, or stakeholder handling. It should not create a spectator gallery.
- Record who was escalated, why, and what decision or action followed.
- Pair with the severity model, incident role roster, and stakeholder update template.

## Example

A data-integrity incident affecting invoices may require finance, legal, customer support, and executive awareness even if the technical fix is simple.

# People Onboarding Plan

Use this template to plan onboarding for a new employee, internal transfer, promoted manager, contractor, or role change.

This is broader than a team checklist. It covers outcomes, context, manager support, role expectations, relationships, feedback, development, and first useful contributions. Access provisioning is necessary, but if that is the whole onboarding plan, congratulations: you have invented paperwork with a start date.

## 30 / 60 / 90-Day Checkpoints

### 30-Day Checkpoint

- What is clear?
- What is confusing?
- What useful contribution has been made?
- What support is missing?
- Are expectations still realistic?
- What should change in the plan?

### 60-Day Checkpoint

- Is the person building the right relationships?
- Are role expectations turning into real work?
- Are feedback and coaching happening early enough?
- Are any risks emerging around workload, confidence, context, or fit?
- What should be adjusted?

### 90-Day Checkpoint

- Is the person operating with appropriate independence for the role?
- Are manager expectations and actual work aligned?
- What development goals should be set next?
- What onboarding content was missing or stale?
- What should be improved before the next person joins?

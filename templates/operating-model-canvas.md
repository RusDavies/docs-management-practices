# Operating Model Canvas

## Purpose

Describe how a recurring service, function, workflow, product operation, or capability actually runs: purpose, ownership, customers, intake, workflow, controls, systems, capacity, metrics, exceptions, and improvement loops.

## Usage Notes

- Use this for operations that are important enough to need real ownership, not for every microscopic task in the organizational sock drawer.
- Name actual owners, systems, thresholds, and review forums. “Handled by the team” is a lovely phrase for hiding accountability.
- Revisit after incidents, launches, major staffing changes, vendor changes, or recurring exceptions.

## Example

A customer onboarding operation might map sales-to-implementation handoff, customer-success ownership, access provisioning, data migration, training, support readiness, go-live criteria, escalation paths, and onboarding health metrics.

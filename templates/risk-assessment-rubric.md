## Usage Notes

- Use the smallest useful rating model. High/Medium/Low with rationale often beats a five-by-five matrix cosplaying as science.
- Include confidence. A high-impact risk with weak evidence may need investigation before mitigation or acceptance.
- Use domain-specific impact measures when money hides the real exposure.

## Example

A stale access-review process may be rated High because inappropriate privileged access could persist, confidence is Medium based on manual owner mappings and audit findings, and treatment is to update owner mapping before the next review cycle.

## Usage Notes

- Use evidence, not unsupported red/yellow/green labels.
- Separate adoption, value, support, relationship, commercial, and product-fit signals.
- Review before renewal pressure turns preventable issues into theatre.

## Example

A customer may have high product usage but repeated unresolved support escalations. Record adoption as strong, support experience as at risk, relationship sentiment as strained, and assign a recovery action owner.

## Usage Notes

- Use escalation to request a decision, unblock a dependency, or surface accepted risk beyond current authority.
- Bring options and a recommendation, not just distress noises in business casual.
- State the deadline and consequence of no decision.
- Send only to people who can help decide or unblock.

## Example

Escalate a vendor contract delay by stating launch impact, options to delay/renegotiate/use interim workaround, recommended path, decision owner, and the date after which customer commitment is at risk.

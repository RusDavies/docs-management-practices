## Usage Notes

- Use metrics to find patterns and improve capability, not to punish people for declaring incidents.
- Pair trend metrics with narrative review. Numbers without context are just confidence cosplay.
- Include corrective-action follow-through; otherwise the dashboard measures pain and ignores healing.

## Example

A quarterly dashboard might show fewer outages but slower detection, several overdue corrective actions, and repeated vendor-related incidents, prompting investment in monitoring and supplier escalation changes.
